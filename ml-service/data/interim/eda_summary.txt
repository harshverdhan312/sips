SIPS Placement Prediction - EDA Summary
=======================================

Dataset
-------
Rows: 2966
Columns: 8
Target: PlacedOrNot
Placed: 1639 (55.26%)
Not placed: 1327 (44.74%)
Missing values: None

Main Findings
-------------
1. CGPA is the strongest individual predictor observed during EDA.
   Pearson correlation with PlacedOrNot: 0.589.

2. Placement rate by CGPA:
   CGPA 5: 7.29%
   CGPA 6: 32.37%
   CGPA 7: 29.50%
   CGPA 8: 100.00%
   CGPA 9: 100.00%

3. The 100% placement rate for CGPA 8 and 9 occurs across every Stream.
   This unusually deterministic pattern is an important dataset limitation
   and must be considered when evaluating model performance.

4. Internships show meaningful association with placement.
   0 internships: 49.14%
   1 internship: 53.65%
   2 internships: 80.57%
   3 internships: 80.39%
   The 3-internship group is small and should not be overinterpreted.

5. Stream shows moderate differences in placement rates.
   ECE and IT have the highest observed rates, while Civil and Mechanical
   have lower observed rates. Stream may interact with CGPA.

6. Age, Hostel, and HistoryOfBacklogs show weak individual associations
   with placement, but they are retained for later model evaluation because
   weak univariate association does not imply no predictive value.

7. Predictor-to-predictor linear correlations are low.
   The strongest absolute numeric predictor correlation is Age-CGPA: 0.120.
   No obvious severe numeric multicollinearity was observed.

8. Duplicate sensitivity is important.
   Total rows: 2966
   Unique complete rows: 1137
   Overall placement rate:
     All rows: 55.26%
     Unique complete rows: 57.96%
   Duplicate records affect some observed distributions, especially CGPA
   groups 6 and 7. Exact duplicates will not be blindly removed because
   there is no student identifier proving that they represent duplicate
   students. Split strategy must account for repeated predictor profiles.

9. Gender is excluded from production model inputs and retained only for
   fairness auditing.
   Female records: 491, placement rate 56.01%
   Male records: 2475, placement rate 55.11%
   Outcome rates are similar, but representation is substantially unequal.

Important Interpretation
------------------------
EDA identifies associations, not causal relationships.
No feature will be removed solely because of low univariate correlation.
Model performance must be compared against simple baselines, including the
strong CGPA-based pattern observed in this dataset.
The dataset should be treated as a proof-of-concept placement dataset rather
than evidence for universal real-world placement behavior.
